# FRIGNANI'S — Brand Concept & Development Briefing

This skill contains the official brand briefing, architectural guidelines, and strict rules for developing and maintaining the FRIGNANI'S website.

---

- **Client / Brand:** FRIGNANI'S
- **Proprietária & Gestora:** Sabrina Frignani
- **Desenvolvimento Web:** Guilherme
- **Direção Criativa & Artes:** Camila (Marketing / Criativo)
- **Branding & Comunicação Internacional:** Claude
- **Brand Identity Pillars:** Elegante, premium, italiana, internacional e minimalista em todas as páginas e pontos de contato.

---

## 2. Core Positioning & Communication

### Positioning Statement
> **"FRIGNANI'S — Excelência Italiana, Comércio Internacional"**
> 
> Conectamos produtores italianos diretamente a profissionais e mercados internacionais, promovendo a cultura, a gastronomia e o patrimônio italiano em todo o mundo por meio de operações de importação e exportação diretas.

### Essential Narrative Points
- Ponte institucional entre produtores italianos, parceiros europeus e mercados globais.
- Curadoria e excelência sem intermediários desnecessários.
- Capacidade de curadoria institucional e alcance de rede europeia.

---

## 3. Main Navigation Structure

The main menu must strictly contain **four items in this exact order**:

1. **Home**
2. **Categorias**
3. **Sobre Nós**
4. **Contato**

---

## 4. Page Content & Structure

### 4.1 Home
- **Core Focus:** Strong emphasis on **Importação & Exportação** (Import & Export).
- **Hero Section:** Central visual element is the **mapa-múndi** (world map) artwork created by Camila.
- **Copy & Messaging:**
  - Headline: *FRIGNANI'S — Excelência Italiana, Comércio Internacional*
  - Introductory copy presenting FRIGNANI'S as the bridge connecting Italian producers and European partners to international markets.
- **Visual CTAs / Sections:**
  - Direct pathways/teaser blocks to **Categorias** and **Sobre Nós**.
- **Footer:** Official contact information (see Section 4.4).

### 4.2 Categorias (Products & Curatorship)
Organized strictly by category.

#### Official Categories:
1. **Vinhos** — Prosecco, espumantes italianos, Valdobbiadene e Franciacorta.
2. **Azeite de Oliva** — Italiano e biológico (*o biológico deve ser identificado pelo código de cor verde*).
3. **Trufa (Tartufo)**.
4. **Produtos Orgânicos / Biológicos**.
5. **Luxury European Kitchen & Lifestyle** — Cerâmicas, luminárias e outros artigos de alto padrão para cozinha, restaurantes e lifestyle.
6. **Cerâmicas e Artesanatos de Alto Nível** — Seleção de peças e produtos de excelência de diferentes regiões da Europa.

> [!CAUTION]
> ### Strict Rule: Zero Supplier / Producer Names
> **Never publish individual names of suppliers, wineries, or commercial partners.**
> The website communicates origin, categories, quality, and FRIGNANI'S curatorial excellence. It must present the European network institutionally without ever exposing commercial partner names.

- **Category Layout:** Each category has its dedicated block or subpage, using the respective artwork from Camila with concise descriptive copy and approved supporting imagery.

### 4.3 Sobre Nós
- **Core Narrative:** Brand heritage, international trade vision, and personal origin story.
- **Key Emotional Element:** **Homenagem à avó de Sabrina** (tribute to Sabrina's grandmother) is the heart and emotional soul of this page, requiring visual and narrative prominence.
- **Visual Element:** Central "About Us" artwork by Camila.
- **Tone:** Elegante, premium e emocionalmente marcante.

### 4.4 Contato
- **E-mail:** `info@frignanis.com`
- **Instagram:** Direct link and handle (@handle oficial) as the primary direct contact channel.
- > [!WARNING]
  > ### Strict Rule: No Phone Number
  > **Do not publish phone numbers anywhere on the website.** Instagram acts as the primary real-time communication channel.

## 5. Visual Identity & Assets Guidelines

- **Artworks:** Exclusively use assets provided by Camila in the shared Google Drive folder:
  - *Home:* Mapa-múndi (Importação & Exportação).
  - *Categorias:* Vinhos, Azeite de Oliva, Trufa, Produtos Biológicos, Luxury Kitchen & Lifestyle, Cerâmicas e Artesanatos.
  - *Sobre Nós:* História da marca / Homenagem à avó.
- **No Independent Artwork Creation:** If a specific dimension or asset is missing, request it directly from Camila rather than generating or fabricating unofficial graphic assets.
- **Color Palette:**
  - **Navy Blue (Azul-marinho):** Dominant brand backdrop / elegance.
  - **Gold (Dourado):** Premium accents, typography highlights, fine borders.
  - **Green (Verde):** Dedicated accent color specifically identifying organic / biological products (azeite biológico, produtos orgânicos).
- **Logotype:** Use official vector/SVG files suited for dark and light backgrounds.

---

## 6. Development & Quality Checklist

Before completing or reviewing any page, verify:
- [ ] Navigation menu strictly matches: **Home**, **Categorias**, **Sobre Nós**, **Contato**.
- [ ] Home prominently highlights **Importação & Exportação** with the world map visual.
- [ ] All product content is organized **by category only**.
- [ ] **NO supplier, producer, or winery names** appear anywhere on the site.
- [ ] "Sobre Nós" gives emotional and narrative prominence to the **grandmother's tribute**.
- [ ] Contact shows **info@frignanis.com** and **Instagram**, with **NO phone numbers**.
- [ ] Biological/organic items utilize the **green brand accent**.
- [ ] Aesthetic is consistently **elegant, premium, Italian, international, and minimalist**.
