FRIGNANI'S is an international trading house, born from an authentic Italian heritage story.

◆ ◆ ◆

Over a century ago, a transatlantic voyage began. On board: an Italian family, ancestral traditions, and the promise to bring the authentic flavors of Italy to the world. That story is our story.

Today, this promise continues: connecting Italian producers directly to discerning markets worldwide, and exporting the finest authentic terroir.

OUR MISSION
To share and elevate Italian gastronomic culture across the globe through the direct import and export of exceptional products and culinary experiences.

Distribution & Commercial Channels:

Direct trade, B2B digital marketplaces, retail partnerships, international food fairs, and specialized trade exhibitions.

OUR PRODUCTS

Italian Gastronomy & Artisanal Terroir

A curated selection of agri-food excellence embodying authentic Italian craftsmanship.

VINS & SPARKLING · WINES & PROSECCO

Exceptional vintages and sparkling selections, sourced directly from certified viticultural partner regions.

OLIVE OIL & FINE GROCERY

Extra virgin olive oils, truffle condiments, and artisanal preserves, curated with exacting standards.

LIFESTYLE & ART DE VIVRE

Tableware, crystal glassware, ambient dining lighting, and bespoke hospitality accessories.

Services: international trade advisory, end-to-end logistics, and customs documentation.

OUR MISSION

A bridge between Italy and the world.

Frignani's was founded on a simple belief: that the finest Italian products deserve a direct path to international markets. We work exclusively with Italian producers, operating through transparent import & export transactions that honour their craft.
