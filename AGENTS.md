# FRIGNANI'S Development Workspace Rules

This repository contains the WordPress theme for **FRIGNANI'S** — Italian Excellence & International Trade.

## Brand Briefing & Skill Reference
Refer to the dedicated skill [`frignanis-brand-concept`](.agents/skills/frignanis-brand-concept/SKILL.md) for complete briefing details.

## Golden Rules
1. **Menu Navigation Structure:** Must strictly follow:
   `Home` · `Categorias` · `Sobre Nós` · `Contato`
2. **Import & Export Positioning:** The homepage must strongly highlight "Importação & Exportação" with Camila's world map artwork as the centerpiece.
3. **No Producer/Supplier/Winery Names:** Communicate exclusively by **categories** (Vinhos, Azeite de Oliva, Trufas, Produtos Biológicos, Luxury Kitchen & Lifestyle, Cerâmicas e Artesanatos Europeus). Never disclose individual suppliers, wineries, or partner names.
4. **Contact Info:** Use `info@frignanis.com` and the official Instagram profile. **Never display telephone numbers.**
5. **Sobre Nós:** Central emotional focus is the tribute to Sabrina's grandmother alongside brand heritage.
6. **Colors:** Navy blue, gold accents, and green code for organic/biological items.
7. **Artworks:** Only use official assets from Camila's shared folder; do not generate unauthorized graphic art.
