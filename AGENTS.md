# FRIGNANI'S Development Workspace Rules

This repository contains the WordPress theme for **FRIGNANI'S IMPORT & EXPORT** — A ponte entre a Itália e o mundo (Luxembourg, September 2026).

## Brand Briefing & Skill Reference
Refer to the dedicated skills:
- [`frignanis-brand-concept`](.agents/skills/frignanis-brand-concept/SKILL.md) for complete briefing details, official texts, and design requirements.
- [`frignanis-color-palette`](.agents/skills/frignanis-color-palette/SKILL.md) for the official color style guide, CSS tokens, WCAG contrast ratios, and UI component styling rules.

## Golden Rules
1. **Menu Navigation Structure:** Must strictly follow:
   `Home` · `Categorias` · `Sobre Nós` · `Contato` (with language selector: PT, EN, FR, IT, ES, DE).
2. **Import & Export Positioning:** The website must strongly highlight "Importação & Exportação / Gestão de Negócios e Intermediação Comercial", with Camila's world map artwork as the centerpiece.
3. **No Producer/Supplier/Winery Names:** Communicate exclusively by **categories** (Vinhos e proseccos de terroir, Iguarias gourmet e biológicas, Art decor / Cooking Supreme). Never disclose individual suppliers, wineries, or partner names.
4. **Official Contact Info:** Use `info@frignanis.com`, `+352 621 766 684` (Telefone e WhatsApp), Luxembourg headquarters, and official Instagram handle `@frignanisgourmet` (https://www.instagram.com/frignanisgourmet).
5. **Sobre Nós:** Central focus is the noble family history from Ferrara (1626 Baronia de Frignani e Frignestani created by King Philip IV), Atlantic crossing to Brazil (Vitória/ES port), international career in Spain, and returning to roots in Luxembourg with support from Federazione Italiana Cuochi – Delegazione Lussemburgo.
6. **Official Color Palette:** 
   - Azul Marinho Real: `#14294A`
   - Dourado Frignani: `#A8843A`
   - Marfim: `#F6F1E6`
   - Verde Itália: `#2E7D4F` (Bandeira italiana / Produtos biológicos)
   - Vermelho Itália: `#B3261E` (Faixa da bandeira italiana)
7. **Key Phrases:** "Não vendemos produtos. Conectamos excelências." · "A ponte entre a Itália e o mundo" · "Um único interlocutor. A Itália inteira ao seu alcance." · "A Itália autêntica, conduzida às mesas mais exigentes do mundo."
8. **Institutional Folder:** Provide an area / button for downloading the institutional presentation folder in PDF.
