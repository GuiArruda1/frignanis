ABOUT US

Our Italian heritage

Rooted in Ferrara, Italy, the Frignani family carried its culinary traditions to Brazil, passing a love of Italian culture from one generation to the next.

Raised in Vitória, Espírito Santo, Sabrina Frignani brings this family heritage and her international experience to a new chapter of the Frignani story.

A STORY SPANNING GENERATIONS, CONTINENTS AND FLAVOURS

The Frignani name carries a history spanning centuries. On August 30, 1626, in Valencia, King Philip IV created the Barony of Frignani and Frignestani, granted to Gaspar Palavicino y Centurión, leaving a historical record that forms part of the name’s journey and tradition.

Generations later, beside one of Brazil’s great ports in Vitória, Espírito Santo, Sabrina Frignani grew up between the family dining table and the rhythm of ships connecting continents. It was in this environment, suspended between Italian roots and an international vocation, that a worldview grounded in connection, trade, and excellence was born.

Following a distinguished international career in Spain, Sabrina united her heritage with extensive experience in management and international trade, inaugurating a new chapter in the family history.

A JOURNEY BACK TO OUR ROOTS

Today, the new Frignani generation makes the journey back.

From Luxembourg, the heart of Europe, Frignani’s was created with the purpose of connecting producers, brands, restaurants, and markets through an international import and export service crafted with proximity, deep knowledge, and meticulous attention to detail.

With the support of the Federazione Italiana Cuochi – Delegazione Lussemburgo, Frignani’s establishes a bridge between Italian tradition and the contemporary world, curating producers and partners that embody quality, authenticity, and excellence.

More than simply transporting products across borders, we believe in building lasting connections between people, cultures, and businesses.
