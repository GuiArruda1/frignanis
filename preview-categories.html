What we
export

Five categories of Italian excellence — selected, documented, and delivered to the world's most discerning buyers. We communicate by category, never by individual supplier or producer name.

I DOC · DOCG · SPUMANTE

Wines & Prosecco

From the rolling hills of Veneto to the volcanic soils of Sicily, our wine selection spans the full breadth of Italian viticulture. We specialise in Prosecco DOC and DOCG, alongside still and sparkling wines curated for the international professional buyer.

II ITALIAN & BIOLOGICAL

Olive Oil

Extra virgin olive oil of Italian origin, available in both conventional and certified biological varieties. Biological products are identified by our green designation — a commitment to traceability and organic farming practices.

BIOLOGICAL
III TARTUFO · SEASONAL HARVEST

Truffle

The truffle — Tartufo — is among Italy's most prized culinary treasures. We source seasonal harvests from certified Italian regions, offering both fresh and preserved formats for restaurants, delicatessens, and luxury food distributors worldwide.

IV CERTIFIED ORGANIC PRODUCTS

Organic / Biological

A growing selection of certified organic Italian products — from grains and legumes to condiments and preserves. All biological products carry the official EU organic certification and are identified by our green designation across the catalog.

BIOLOGICAL
V LAMPS · CERAMICS · LIFESTYLE ARTICLES

Luxury European Kitchen & Lifestyle

Beyond food and wine, Frignani's curates a selection of luxury European articles for the kitchen and restaurant environment — handcrafted ceramics, artisan lighting, and bespoke lifestyle accessories that bring Italian and European craftsmanship to the table.
